# Adding Generation 2 Pokémon to Claude Red — research

Research only; nothing here is built yet. Scope: the 100 Johto Pokémon (#152–251) woven into the existing Kanto
game — wild encounters, NPC trainers, gym leaders, Elite Four, rival, static/legendary encounters — without adding
Johto itself.

## TL;DR

- **The data side is mechanical.** Species, learnsets, moves and evolutions can be pulled from
  [pret/pokecrystal](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal), which uses the same assembly layout that
  `tools/convert_data.js` already parses from pokered.
- **The art is the biggest job.** Every Pokémon is a hand-built vector sprite in code (`src/data/mons/*.js`, about 1.9 KB
  per species). That means 100 new definitions, roughly 190 KB of hand-tuned shapes.
- **The engine needs real new systems.** Gen 2 species depend on things Red doesn't have: Dark/Steel types, split
  Special, happiness evolutions, held-item trade evolutions and ~86 new moves. How much of that to port is the main
  decision (see [Decisions](#decisions-to-make-first)).
- **Integration touches almost everything:** 57 wild tables, 391 trainer teams across 47 classes, 334 trainer objects,
  static encounters, gifts, the Pokédex UI, share cards, the quiz, the Battle Tower and the save validation.
- **Things that come for free:** cries are procedural per species (`G.cry` in `src/core/audio.js`), Battle Tower pools filter
  by stats, save files store species by name, and the battle fuzzer picks random species. New species slot into all
  four automatically.

## Gen 2 mechanics the new Pokémon depend on

### New types: Dark and Steel
Needed by Umbreon, Murkrow, Sneasel, Houndour/Houndoom, Tyranitar (Dark) and Steelix, Scizor, Skarmory,
Forretress (Steel). Retcons to Gen 1 Pokémon: Magnemite/Magneton become Electric/Steel. Clefairy stays Normal
(Fairy is Gen 6).
Gen 2 also fixed Gen 1's type chart: Ghost does 2x to Psychic, Bug does 0.5x to Poison, Poison does 1x to Bug,
and Ice does 0.5x to Fire.
**Choice:** full Gen 2 chart (balanced, recommended once Dark/Steel exist) or Gen 1 chart plus the two new types (keeps
the original's quirks).

### The Special split
Gen 2 splits Special into Sp. Atk and Sp. Def for **every** Pokémon, Gen 1 included. Many Gen 2 Pokémon only make
sense split, e.g. Blissey (SpA 75 / SpD 135) and Shuckle (10 / 230).
- **Port the split:** `calcStat` for `spa/spd`, special moves use `spa` vs `spd`, stat stages (Amnesia, Growth), Volcano Badge
  boost, X Special, the summary screen, Battle Tower movesets, save migration (existing saves copy `spc` into
  both; Gen 2 also shares one DV between them).
- **Keep single Special:** squash each Gen 2 Pokémon to one number (e.g. SpA or the average). Cheaper, but it distorts
  the Gen 2 roster.

### Evolution methods
| Method | Pokémon |
|---|---|
| Happiness (new per-Pokémon stat, needs a save field) | Golbat→**Crobat**, Chansey→**Blissey**, Pichu, Cleffa, Igglybuff, Togepi |
| Happiness + time of day | Eevee→**Espeon** (day) / **Umbreon** (night) |
| Trade holding an item | Onix→**Steelix** & Scyther→**Scizor** (Metal Coat), Seadra→**Kingdra** (Dragon Scale), Poliwhirl→**Politoed** & Slowpoke→**Slowking** (King's Rock), Porygon→**Porygon2** (Up-Grade) |
| Sun Stone (new item) | Gloom→**Bellossom**, Sunkern→Sunflora |
| Stat comparison at Lv20 | Tyrogue→Hitmonlee / Hitmonchan / **Hitmontop** |

Trades in this game are link-based (Social Zone). Held-item trade evolutions therefore need either held items
carried in trade links, or a single-player alternative. Gen 1 remakes commonly use "use the item on it".

### Moves
~86 new moves (Crunch, Faint Attack, Iron Tail, Steel Wing, Sacred Fire, Aeroblast, Protect, Curse, Spikes, Baton
Pass, Rain Dance/Sunny Day, Sandstorm, Hidden Power…). Gen 2 learnsets lean on them. There are three options:
1. **Port all.** This means new effect handlers plus systems: weather, held items, entry hazards, Protect/Endure,
   Baton Pass, Hidden Power typing.
2. **Port the damaging moves and simple effects** (roughly two-thirds), and remap the rest in learnsets to the nearest Gen 1 move.
3. **Gen 1 moves only:** rewrite every Gen 2 learnset with existing moves. Cheapest, but Pokémon lose their signature
   moves (no Sacred Fire for Ho-Oh, no Aeroblast for Lugia).
New TMs also mean deciding whether Gen 1 Pokémon get Gen 2 TM compatibility.

### Held items
Needed for trade evolutions. Also where Gen 2 balance lives (Leftovers, type boosters, berries). The minimum viable
version is evolution items only, with no in-battle held effects.

### Breeding and babies
Pichu, Cleffa, Igglybuff, Smoochum, Elekid, Magby, Tyrogue and Togepi come from eggs in Gen 2. The current Day Care
is Gen 1 style (one Pokémon, no eggs). Options: build egg breeding (two-slot Day Care, compatibility, egg moves, a
hatch step counter; the step hook already exists at `extra.js:7`), or make babies gifts and wild encounters.

## Placing the new Pokémon by caliber

Gen 1 stat totals (5-stat, as stored) run from 175 to 590 with a median of 345. The rule of thumb: put a Gen 2 Pokémon
where a Gen 1 Pokémon of similar stat total and evolution stage already appears. Game Freak's own Gen 2 Kanto
tables (`data/wild/kanto_*.asm` in pokecrystal) are the best reference for which species fit which routes. Their
levels are post-game, so keep Red's level slots and swap only the species.

| Tier | Examples | Where / when |
|---|---|---|
| Early route (base forms, stat total ~200–270) | Sentret, Hoothoot (night), Ledyba (morning), Spinarak (night), Hoppip, Sunkern, Natu | Routes 1–3, 22, 24–25, Viridian Forest |
| Mid route | Marill, Mareep, Wooper, Aipom, Yanma, Snubbull, Teddiursa, Slugma, Swinub, Remoraid, Chinchou (Super Rod) | Routes 4–11, Mt. Moon, Rock Tunnel, water routes |
| Evolved / uncommon | Flaaffy, Quagsire, Furret, Noctowl, Girafarig, Dunsparce, Qwilfish, Corsola, Mantine, Delibird | Routes 12–21, Safari Zone, Seafoam |
| Strong, one per area | Heracross, Pinsir-like Safari rares, Skarmory, Houndour, Sneasel, Misdreavus, Murkrow, Gligar, Stantler | Safari Zone, Victory Road, Pokémon Mansion, Pokémon Tower |
| Pseudo-legendary | Larvitar → Pupitar → Tyranitar (600) | Cerulean Cave, very rare; Pupitar in Victory Road at most |
| Starters | Chikorita, Cyndaquil, Totodile | Gifts, e.g. an Oak's-aide gift after N badges, like Bill's Eevee |
| Babies | Pichu, Cleffa, Igglybuff, Togepi, Smoochum, Elekid, Magby, Tyrogue | Eggs if breeding is built; otherwise gifts (Togepi egg from Oak's aide, Tyrogue at the Fighting Dojo) |
| Evolution-only | Crobat, Steelix, Scizor, Kingdra, Blissey, Politoed, Slowking, Porygon2, Espeon, Umbreon, Bellossom, Ampharos, Tyranitar, Meganium… | Never wild in the main game; reached by evolution. Trainers can carry them late. |

## Everything else that must change

- **Pokédex UI:** 151 is hard-coded at `menus.js:154,166`. It needs 251 entries and possibly a Kanto/National toggle.
  `dexOrder` needs extending.
- **Share cards and milestones:** `share.js:19,51,345` (`/151`, "Pokédex complete").
- **Who's That Pokémon quiz:** `wtp.js:11` filters `dex <= 151`. Include Gen 2 or add a mode.
- **Save compatibility:** species are stored by name, so old saves keep loading. Happiness and held items need new
  fields with defaults. A split Special needs a one-time migration in `G.loadSave` / `Mon.from`. Import
  validation (`savetransfer.js validSave`) accepts any known species, so it works as-is.
- **Social Zone links:** trade and team links validate species against `G.DATA`. They should work once the data
  exists, but held items, happiness and split stats need to be carried and validated.
- **Glitches:** leave the Gen 2 species out of `glitches.js`'s internal index so MissingNo. and the Mew trick behave exactly as they
  did.
- **Customizer, portraits, trainer pics:** unaffected.
- **Tests:** `fuzzbattle.js` picks random species, so it covers new Pokémon and moves once they exist. `monsheet.js`
  renders sprite sheets for review. `talkfuzz.js` and `stepfuzz.js` cover new scripts and gifts.

## Suggested phases

1. **Engine:** Dark/Steel and the type chart, new evolution types, happiness, minimal held items, a Special split
   if chosen, and new moves at the chosen tier. Verify with `fuzzbattle.js` using existing species.
2. **Data:** a `tools/convert_data_gen2.js` reading pokecrystal (base stats, learnsets, evolutions, moves), merged
   into `pokedata.js`. Also 100 paraphrased dex entries.
3. **Art:** 100 sprite definitions in new `src/data/mons/152-*.js` files, reviewed with `monsheet.js`. This is the long
   pole and can run in parallel with phase 1.
4. **World:** wild tables (57 maps plus rods), curated trainer edits, gifts, legendaries.
5. **Meta:** Pokédex 251, share and quiz, Battle Tower `LEGENDS`, save migration.
6. **Balance pass:** play through, and run `fuzzbattle`/`talkfuzz`/`stepfuzz`/`warpcheck`.

**Relative size:** art is roughly half the total effort, the engine a quarter, and data + world + meta the rest. A
**Gen 1-mechanics-only** version would roughly halve the engine work, but costs Gen 2 authenticity: no split Special,
Gen 1 moves only, babies as gifts and item-use instead of trade evolutions.

## Decisions to make first

1. **Mechanics fidelity:** full Gen 2 (split Special, new moves, held items, weather) or Gen 1 rules with Gen 2
   Pokémon?
2. **Type chart:** Gen 2 fixes, or keep the Gen 1 quirks with Dark/Steel added?
3. **Breeding:** build eggs, or make babies gifts?
4. **Trade evolutions:** carry held items in trade links, or use a single-player alternative?
5. **Legendaries:** fixed post-game encounters, or build roaming for the beasts?
6. **Pokédex:** one 251 National Dex, or keep the Kanto 151 for "Pokédex complete" and add a National view?
7. **Retcons:** make Magnemite/Magneton Electric/Steel, and give Gen 1 Pokémon Gen 2 moves and TMs?
